The credit line

The widget adds one short line underneath itself, in your page:

Proxy checker by ProxyVerity

Keeping it visible is the condition of use. That is the whole deal: the widget is free, and the credit is what it costs. It is an ordinary line of text with an ordinary link, and it takes your page's own colour and typeface — it inherits them, so it reads as part of your design rather than a sticker on top of it. You can move it above the widget and align it however you like.

What you may not do is hide it, shrink it below legibility or make it transparent. That is not a technical restriction — you could do it in two lines of CSS — it is the one thing we ask in return, and it is also what Google expects of an attribution link.

Colours must be hex — #ff8800 or #f80. Named colours and rgb() are not accepted, and anything we cannot read is replaced by the theme's own value, so a typo leaves you with a working widget rather than a broken one. The credit's text and destination are not attributes and will not become any.

How it behaves

  • Up to 1000 proxies per submission. Lists longer than data-threshold are sent in batches of data-chunk-size, about one batch per second, with progress shown as they go.
  • Results open on /result.php?hash=… — its own address, so it can be bookmarked or shared. The widget does not render results row by row.
  • Checking happens on our servers, so your visitors' browsers never connect to the proxies they are testing.
  • The widget runs in a sandboxed iframe and reports only its own height back to your page. Your page sends it nothing.
  • If a visitor goes faster than the service allows, they see a short wait message and the widget retries by itself.
