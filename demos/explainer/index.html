What happens when you check a proxy

A working checker, embedded below, and a short account of what it does once you press the button. Free to put on your own page.

Three protocols, not one

An address and a port tell you nothing about what speaks on the other end. The same 1.2.3.4:8080 might be HTTP, SOCKS4 or SOCKS5, and a list that labels them is often guessing. So all three are tried, and whichever answers first decides what the proxy is.

Anonymity is measured, not declared

Whether a proxy is transparent, anonymous or elite is not a property it advertises — it is what the far end actually sees. The check goes through the proxy to a judge that reports the headers it received, and the level follows from which of them survived the trip.

Why free proxy lists overstate themselves

A list is a snapshot, and public proxies die quickly — by the time a page is indexed, much of it is already wrong. The only honest number is the one you measure yourself, which is what this tool is for.

Putting it on your page

One script tag, no server, no account. Colours, theme, corner radius and typeface are attributes, so it can be made to look like your site rather than ours. The snippet builder has a live preview, and the reference documentation lists every option.
