A proxy checker that lives on your page

This is one script tag, running live. Paste a few proxies below and they are checked against HTTP, SOCKS4 and SOCKS5 — whichever answers first wins.

What this particular demo shows

The widget takes its colours from attributes, so it does not have to look like somebody else's product on your site. This page uses a teal accent, a ten-pixel radius and the system typeface. Nothing was styled by hand — those are three attributes.

<script src="https://api.proxyverity.com/widget/embed.js"
        async
        data-theme="light"
        data-accent="#0f766e"
        data-radius="10"
        data-font="system"></script>

Colours are validated server-side: anything that is not a plain hex value falls back to the theme's own, because these end up inside a stylesheet. The palette carries over to the results page too, so the look does not break at the moment a visitor is reading their results.

Where the rest lives
